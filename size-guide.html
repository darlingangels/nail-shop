♡ how to measure

  1. Get a flexible measuring tape.
  2. Hold the measuring tape horizontally across the widest part of your natural nail bed.
  3. Measure the width of each nail in millimeters for the most accurate fit.
  4. Write down the measurements so you can compare your nails with the available sizes.
♡ little tip: Measure all ten nails, since your left and right hands may have slightly different nail widths.
